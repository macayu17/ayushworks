Doto-Variable.woff2 is the Latin variable font (weights 100-900) previously served by Google Fonts:
https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/doto/v3/t5t6IRMbNJ6TQG7Il_EKPqP9zTnvqouBWho.woff2
License: doto/OFL.txt. Kept local to avoid an external stylesheet request.

Caveat-Regular.woff2 is generated from caveat/Caveat-Regular.ttf using fontTools:
instantiateVariableFont(font, {'wght': 400}), subset U+0000-00FF and U+2000-206F,
then save with font.flavor = 'woff2'. Original source and license remain in caveat/.
